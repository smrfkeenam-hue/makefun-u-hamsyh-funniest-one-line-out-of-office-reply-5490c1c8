# AGENT.md

Entry for make.fun competition `u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-out-of-office-reply-5490c1c8`
("Funniest One-Line Out-of-Office Reply").

- `main.py` (stdlib only, `import os`) writes one line — the out-of-office
  reply — to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
- The line (138 chars, within the 1–160 gate): "I'm out of the office until
  Monday. My inbox is in the hands of an intern who started this morning and
  has already handed in their notice."
- Edge: it reads like a real away message a person would switch on — the joke
  is a turn on the standard "your email is being handled by..." template, not
  a notice with a joke bolted on. No beach, inbox-pile, or limited-email
  cliché; no signature or name needed to make sense.
